Strategic Partnerships

At Deraedge, we work with a carefully chosen few.

We maintain a deliberately selective approach to strategic relationships. We partner with a limited number of individuals, family offices, institutions and specialist organisations whose capital, expertise, technology, network or strategic objectives align with our long-term direction.

Our relationships are built around more than capital. They are built around alignment, discretion and shared vision.

Important

  • We do not operate a mass-market investment platform.
  • We do not accept open-ended partnership requests.
  • Every partnership is considered independently.

Admission

Partnership with Deraedge is strictly consideration-based.

There is no public membership, automatic acceptance or standard partnership package. Prospective partners are invited to submit an initial profile for consideration. Where there is a potential strategic fit, our team will initiate a private conversation.
