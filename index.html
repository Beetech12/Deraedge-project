Who We Are

Not an investment product. A research institution that trades.

Unlike traditional investment firms, Deraedge does not offer public investment products, manage funds on behalf of the general public, or solicit capital from retail investors. Our activities are conducted exclusively through the deployment of the Company's proprietary capital and, where appropriate, capital contributed by a limited number of approved strategic partners.

We build proprietary market intelligence systems, analytical frameworks, financial technologies and research capabilities that strengthen our competitive advantage in global markets.

Our objective is not simply to participate in the markets, but to understand them more deeply.
